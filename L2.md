3. Why Persona at L2 Is a Bad Idea

If we define persona at L2:

Personas become unstable

Different domains conflict

L3 loses meaning


Example:

Finance L2 → “budget conscious”

Travel L2 → “luxury traveler”

Entertainment L2 → “heavy streamer”


So… what is the persona? 🤷

That’s why:

> L2 = domain behavior
L3 = cross-domain persona
