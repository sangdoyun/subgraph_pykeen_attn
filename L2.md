1️⃣ Event Representation Layer

Goal: Convert raw events into structured feature vectors.

Input

Raw event:

<timestamp, event_type, entity_id, attributes>

Output

Feature vector:

e_t = [
  event_type_embedding,
  entity_embedding,
  time_features,
  context_features
]

Why this matters:

Encodes semantics (meeting, salary, missed call)

Encodes entity (manager vs friend)

Encodes time (weekday/weekend)

Encodes context (home, driving)


This is no longer frequency counting — it’s representation learning.


---

2️⃣ Session Sequence Construction

Goal: Build ordered sequences for modeling.

Process

Segment into sessions (as before)

Convert session into ordered sequence of embeddings:


S = [e1, e2, e3, …]

Now sequences are learnable sequences, not raw logs.


---

3️⃣ Trigger-Centered Sequence Extraction

Instead of enumerating all pairs, we:

Identify trigger events (meeting end, salary credit, etc.)

Extract local sub-sequence window around trigger:


Subsequence = [e_trigger, e_t+1, e_t+2, … e_t+k]

This reduces noise and improves learning.


---

4️⃣ Conditional Next-Action Modeling

This is the core ML step.

We train a lightweight model to estimate:

> P(next_action | trigger + recent context)



Options (mobile-feasible):

Logistic regression

Small MLP

Lightweight transformer (optional)

Temporal convolution

Markov model with learned transition weights


Output:

Probability distribution over possible next actions


This replaces lift-based counting.


---

5️⃣ Sequence Strength Estimation

Instead of lift, compute:

Predicted probability of B after A

Baseline probability of B

Confidence margin = P(B | A) − P(B)


This is model-based, not count-based.

We retain:

Probability margin

Entropy reduction

Calibration score



---

6️⃣ Temporal Consistency Modeling

To ensure robustness:

Evaluate model confidence across multiple occurrences of A

Track variance of prediction confidence


Sequence must:

Produce high confidence repeatedly

Not depend on a single outlier session

7️⃣ Sequence Interpretation Layer

Prediction margin > threshold

Stable across K occurrences

interpretable

8️⃣ Online Adaptation & Decay

To make it robust:

Maintain sliding window training

Apply exponential decay to old observations

Drop pattern if confidence falls below threshold


This handles behavior drift.
