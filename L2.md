L1 Patterns = Raw but Normalized Financial Signals

What L1 Really Is (Final Clarification)

L1 patterns are NOT personas and NOT composite behaviors.
They are:

> Canonical, normalized representations of raw financial signals
that are directly computable and reusable at higher levels.



Examples from your image:

Salary range

Transaction frequency

Credit card usage

Wallet top-up pattern

SIP / EMI presence

Balance alerts


These are features, but expressed as patterns (bands / states).


---

Do We Need Clustering for L1?

❌ No (and this is important)

Clustering at L1 would:

Reduce interpretability

Make downstream reasoning harder

Mix semantics too early


L1 should be rule-light, deterministic, and stable.

Clustering is more appropriate at L2 and L3.


---

Correct L1 Detection Method (Blocks)

L1 = Signal → Aggregation → Normalization → Banding

This is the generic pipeline.


---

Block 1: Signal Identification (from image)

Input raw signals such as:

Transactions

Notifications

Bank / wallet / card identifiers

Calendar-linked payments


This step is schema-driven, not ML.


---

Block 2: Temporal Aggregation

Each L1 signal has a natural aggregation window:

Signal	Window

Salary credit	Monthly
Transaction count	Daily / Weekly
Spend amount	Weekly / Monthly
Credit card bill	Monthly
SIP / EMI	Monthly
Wallet top-ups	Weekly
Alerts	Daily


This is not heuristic — it’s domain-consistent.


---

Block 3: Metric Extraction

For each signal, compute a single scalar or small set:

Examples:

Salary → monthly credit amount

Txn frequency → txns per day (avg)

Credit usage → % txns via credit card

Wallet usage → top-ups per week

SIP → count / amount per month

Alerts → alerts per day


At this point, everything is still numeric.


---

Block 4: Normalization (Critical)

Normalize per user or population-wide, depending on signal:

Salary → population-based (income proxy)

Txn/spend → per-user (behavioral)

Alerts → per-user


Methods:

Percentiles

Z-score

Log scaling


This removes absolute bias.


---

Block 5: Banding / Discretization (This Creates L1 Patterns)

Convert normalized values into semantic bands.

Examples:

Salary

Salary_Low

Salary_Mid

Salary_High


Transaction Frequency

Txn_Low

Txn_Medium

Txn_High


Credit Usage

Credit_None

Credit_Low

Credit_High


Wallet

Wallet_None

Wallet_Occasional

Wallet_Heavy


SIP / EMI

SIP_None

SIP_Present


These bands ARE the L1 patterns.


---

Why Banding Works Better Than Clustering at L1

Banding	Clustering

Explainable	Hard to interpret
Stable over time	Sensitive to noise
Reusable	Cluster-dependent
Cheap (on-device)	Costly
Domain-aligned	Abstract


Your image clearly assumes banded L1 patterns, not latent clusters.
