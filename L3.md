Here is the Travel Pattern Detection table, structured exactly like your Finance example.
Concept:
 * L1 (State): The "atomic" travel events or states (e.g., booking a flight, visiting a new city).
 * L2 (Behavior): The habit revealed by combining L1 states (e.g., frequent short trips = "Business Traveler Behavior").
 * L3 (Persona): The final identity assigned to the user (e.g., "Corporate Road Warrior").
Travel Pattern Detection Framework
| Level | Component | Description & Examples | Detection Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Travel State (Raw Patterns) | Definition: Discrete, repetitive travel-related events or status indicators detected from raw logs.

Examples:
• Commute Pattern: Daily Home \leftrightarrow Work (Time/Distance)
• Long-Distance Trip: Crossing city/state boundaries (>100km)
• Booking Signal: Flight/Train/Bus ticket SMS or App usage
• Stay Signal: Hotel check-in or "Night away from Home" location
• Transport Mode: High speed movement (Train/Car) vs. Walking | Heuristic Engine (Periodicity & Context)
Logic: Detect recurrence of location changes or specific keywords (PNR, Check-in, Boarding) in notifications.
Example: "If 'PNR' keyword found AND Location changes > 500km \rightarrow Flag as 'Trip State'." |
| L2 | Travel Behavior (Composite) | Definition: Meaningful grouping of L1 states that reveals how and why the user travels.

Examples:
• Business Travel Behavior: (L1) Weekday Flights + (L1) Short 1-2 day Hotel Stays + (L1) Cab Usage.
• Leisure/Vacation Behavior: (L1) Weekend/Holiday Dates + (L1) Resort Location + (L1) Multi-person bookings.
• Backpacker Behavior: (L1) Bus/Train bookings + (L1) Hostel/Budget stay + (L1) Long duration.
• Daily Commuter: (L1) Fixed Mon-Fri route + (L1) Public Transport/Metro Card usage. | Unsupervised Clustering (DBSCAN / K-Means)
Logic: Create a feature vector (e.g., [TripFrequency, AvgDuration, WeekendRatio, BookingClass]). Cluster users with similar vectors to identify common behaviors like "Weekend Warriors" or "Daily Grinders." |
| L3 | Travel Persona (Identity) | Definition: The semantic identity of the user based on their dominant L2 behaviors. Used for targeting.

Examples:
• CORPORATE_ROAD_WARRIOR: High frequency business travel behavior.
• DIGITAL_NOMAD: Long-term stays in varying locations, non-standard commute.
• FAMILY_VACATIONER: Seasonal leisure trips, multiple distinct passengers.
• ADVENTURE_SEEKER: Trekking/Remote locations, budget travel behavior.
• HOMEBODY: Very low L1 Trip frequency, strong local commute pattern only. | Classification / Rules (Decision Tree)
Logic: Map L2 Clusters to named Personas using business rules.
Example: "IF Cluster == 'High Freq Short Trips' AND Spend == 'Premium' THEN Persona = 'CORPORATE_ROAD_WARRIOR'." |
How to Proposal This:
 * L1 is the "Sensor": It tells you where they are and how they move (State).
 * L2 is the "Interpreter": It tells you what kind of trip it is (Behavior).
 * L3 is the "Labeler": It tells you who they are (Persona).
